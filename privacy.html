Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Trail Escape is an arrow puzzle game intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over, published by Madiha Dawood under the Taimberry Games brand. This policy covers the current ad-free game, including Trail Escape Test.

What stays on your device

The game saves completed levels, unfinished puzzles, play times, sound, music, vibration, reduced-motion settings and whether you have seen the tutorial. These are used to continue your game and remember your choices. They are not sent to a game server.

What we do not collect through the game

There are no accounts, ads, analytics services or remote crash-reporting services in this version. The game does not request your name, age, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. We do not sell your gameplay information.

Clearing your progress

Settings → Reset all progress clears completed levels, unfinished puzzles and play times, but keeps your preferences. Android’s Clear storage option removes the app’s saved progress and preferences. Uninstalling also removes local app data. Browser previews use browser storage, which you can clear in your browser settings. There is no cloud save or recovery service. The normal and test apps keep separate saves.

Younger players and privacy

The game is not intended for children under 13. We do not collect or verify players’ ages. The current game has no chat, accounts, advertisements or purchases. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to help with support requests. Please do not send unnecessary personal details, photos or sensitive information. If you believe a child’s personal information has been sent to us, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

If you contact support

Only if you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to answer your request and keep them only as needed for support and any applicable legal obligations. Email is handled by the email providers involved. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of support correspondence; some information may need to be retained where required by law.

Other services

Your device, app store and email provider operate their own services under their own privacy notices. The game does not control data you provide directly to those services.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, we will update this policy. Any future ads or online features will need their own disclosures and any required consent before they operate.
