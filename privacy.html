Taimberry Games · Trail Escape

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Trail Escape is an arrow puzzle game published by Madiha Dawood under the Taimberry Games brand and intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over. This policy covers Trail Escape and Trail Escape Test. Features can differ between builds. The current closed-testing build uses Google’s sample test-ad units only, not live AdMob ads. Some builds do not show ads. Other ad-supported builds may show a banner on the level-selection screen and occasional interstitial ads at level-completion breaks. Ads are not intended to interrupt an active puzzle.

How to delete your data

Trail Escape has no player accounts and does not upload game saves to our servers. Puzzle progress, play times and preferences are stored on your device.

  1. To reset completed levels, unfinished puzzles and play times, open Settings in the game and choose Reset all progress. Your preferences remain.
  2. To delete all Trail Escape data from an Android device, open the device’s Settings, find Trail Escape under Apps, and choose Clear storage or Clear data. You can also uninstall the game. Menu names may differ by device. For a browser preview, clear this site’s stored data in your browser settings.
  3. To request deletion of support correspondence, email taimberrygames@gmail.com from the address you used to contact us and write “Delete my Trail Escape support email” in the subject. We keep support emails only as needed to handle your request and for applicable legal obligations. We will delete correspondence we hold when we can, except information we are required to retain by law. Email providers may retain copies under their own policies.

In ad-supported builds, Google AdMob handles ad-related data described below under Google’s privacy terms. We do not store this ad data on a Trail Escape server and cannot delete records held by Google or other advertising providers. You can review your advertising identifier in your Android device’s privacy or advertising settings and read the Google Privacy Policy.

Game progress and settings

The game saves completed levels, unfinished puzzles, play times, sound, music, vibration, reduced-motion settings and whether you have seen the tutorial on your device. These are used to continue your game and remember your choices. They are not sent to a Trail Escape game server. There is no account or cloud save.

Settings → Reset all progress clears completed levels, unfinished puzzles and play times, but keeps your preferences. Android’s Clear storage option or uninstalling removes the app’s local data. Browser previews use browser storage, which you can clear in your browser settings. The normal and test apps keep separate saves.

Advertising and Google AdMob

In ad-supported builds, Trail Escape uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to request and display ads. Google says this SDK may automatically collect and share an IP address, app interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID. Google's AdMob SDK may also use your device's IP address to estimate an approximate location, used only to serve regionally relevant ads. Google describes these data types and their uses for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention in its Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK.

Google and participating advertising technology providers may use ad-request information to deliver, measure and protect advertising. Whether an ad is personalized can depend on your region, privacy choices, device settings and applicable requirements. We do not operate our own game server or add a separate game analytics or crash-reporting service. Google's handling of information is also described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and privacy choices

Ad-supported Android builds use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check privacy choices and show any consent message required before requesting ads. The European regulations message is configured for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Where the app is required to provide a privacy-options entry point, it will be available in the app. Your choices may affect whether personalized, non-personalized or limited ads can be requested.

You can also review or reset your advertising identifier in your Android device’s privacy or advertising settings.

Children and younger players

Trail Escape is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. The game does not ask for or verify your age. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to help with support requests. Please do not send unnecessary personal details, photos or sensitive information. If you believe a child’s personal information has been sent to us, contact us so we can review it and take appropriate steps.

If you contact support

Only if you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to answer your request and keep them only as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. Email is handled by the email providers involved.

Other services

Your device, app store and email provider operate their own services under their own privacy notices. The game does not control information you provide directly to those services.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the game’s data practices change. Please check the public policy page for the latest version.
